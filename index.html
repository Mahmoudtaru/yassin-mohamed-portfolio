MY TEACHING PHILOSOPHY

Two Disciplines.
One complete transformation.

No robotic drills. Learning the Quran is about both the tongue’s precision and the soul’s comprehension.

01 / ARTICULATION & PHONETICS

Makharij & Tajweed Rigor

Non-native vocal tracts naturally substitute unfamiliar Arabic sounds with English equivalents (like replacing 'ح' with 'H' or 'ع' with 'A'). I break down tongue placement, breath pressure, and resonance points using anatomically clear guides so you speak with authentic Arabic clarity.

Pinpoint throat & tongue positioning drills
Real-time audio spectrogram visual feedback
Gentle, patient correction without frustration
02 / MIND & SPIRIT

Tadabbur & Arabic Immersion

Recitation without meaning remains incomplete. In each lesson, we examine key words from the Ayah, contextual background (Asbab al-Nuzul), and how to reflect on these timeless verses during your daily prayers (Salah).

Bilingual explanations suited for Western mindsets
Practical takeaways to elevate Khushoo' in prayer
Personalized homework flashcards & revision sheets
